We Started With a Treehouse and a Dream

WildNest began as a single hand-built treehouse on a family plot of highland forest in Benguet. The founder, Mang Ricardo — a former forest ranger — wanted one simple target: a place where visitors could sleep inside the forest, not outside it.

We didn't build a structured resort and plant landscaping around it. We carefully engineered around living structures, matching the pristine flow of the local geography.
